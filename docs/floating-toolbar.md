# Use the Plyph floating toolbar on Windows

Select text in an app such as Notepad. If Plyph shows its logo button, click it to reveal your actions. Choose **Correct**, **Rewrite**, **Ask**, or **More actions**. Review the result and use **Copy** or **Replace** when available.

**Ask** can answer a question or carry out a request. If you opened it with text selected, you can clear **Include selected text** to ask without that context. You can also open **Ask…** from the Plyph tray menu.

## Make the toolbar your own

In **Settings → Toolbar**, choose a horizontal row or a vertical icon strip under **Toolbar orientation**. In vertical mode, **More actions** opens a labeled menu beside the strip. You can pin up to four frequently used actions under **Pinned actions**.

Use **Floating toolbar placement** to choose **Beside selected text (if supported)** or **Near mouse cursor**. If the toolbar doesn’t appear in an app, check that **Show floating selection toolbar** is enabled and the app is absent from **Excluded apps**. You can also assign an **Open actions** shortcut under **Settings → Shortcuts & Capture**.
